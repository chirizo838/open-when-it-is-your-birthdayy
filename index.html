Finding Our Way Back

For us. For what we were. For what we might still become.

“Some love doesn’t end. It pauses — waiting for the right moment to speak again.”

This is not an apology. And it is not a promise.

It is an open door.

We walked away carrying unfinished sentences and feelings that never learned how to end. Time passed, and in that time, I learned — about myself, about love, about what truly matters.

I don’t believe in going back to who we were. But I do believe in meeting again as who we are now.

If our paths cross once more, I hope it is not out of loneliness, but choice. Not out of habit, but understanding.

And if that moment comes, I’ll be here — not waiting blindly, but ready.

Maybe someday — when time is kinder, and we are too.
